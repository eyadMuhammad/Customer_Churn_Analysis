# Customer_Churn_Analysis
# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis & Insights Dashboard

An end-to-end customer churn analysis project designed to identify high-risk customer segments, uncover key behavioral patterns driving churn, and deliver actionable retention strategies through visual analytics.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Customer churn directly impacts recurring revenue and growth. This project analyzes historical customer data to highlight top retention metrics, pinpoint churn risk factors across different contract types and tenure groups, and present findings in an executive-ready format.

> **Note on Data Availability:** Raw dataset files are omitted from this repository for privacy and data compliance.

## 🔍 Key Findings & Business Insights
1. **Tenure Impact:** Customers in their first 12 months show a 3x higher probability of churn compared to long-term subscribers.
2. **Contract Type:** Month-to-month contract holders account for the vast majority of total churned users.
3. **Payment Methods:** Customers utilizing automatic payment methods exhibit significantly higher retention rates.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations for Retention
* Target new sign-ups with focused onboarding campaigns during the first 90 days.
* Introduce incentives to convert month-to-month subscribers into annual contracts.
